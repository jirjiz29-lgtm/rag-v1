# GypsyCircle – Trip Operations Standard Operating Procedure
Document type: Operations SOP
Status: Proposed workflow – adapt to each departure

## 1. Objective
Deliver each group departure with confirmed services, clear traveller communication and documented operational ownership.

## 2. Pre-trip workflow
1. Confirm destination, dates, route, group size and trip leader.
2. Prepare the itinerary with realistic travel times, rest stops and contingency time.
3. Obtain written supplier confirmations for accommodation, transport, meals and activities.
4. Verify package inclusions, exclusions, taxes, permits and payment milestones.
5. Share the final itinerary, meeting point, packing guidance and emergency contact process with confirmed travellers.
6. Maintain a passenger list and emergency contact details with restricted access.
7. Reconfirm critical suppliers before departure.

## 3. Departure-day checklist
- Verify the vehicle, driver details and pickup point.
- Check traveller attendance against the approved list.
- Share the trip leader's contact details with travellers.
- Brief the group on itinerary, safety expectations and local rules.
- Record delays, substitutions and incidents.

## 4. During the trip
- Track the group and communicate schedule changes.
- Confirm accommodation and transport arrangements before arrival.
- Do not leave a traveller behind without following the group's safety and escalation procedure.
- Report accidents, illness, missing-person concerns or unsafe conditions immediately to the trip lead and management.
- Record supplier failures and customer complaints.

## 5. Post-trip workflow
- Reconcile supplier bills and approved expenses.
- Record feedback and unresolved complaints.
- Document incidents and supplier performance.
- Collect approved trip photos or testimonials only with appropriate consent.
- Prepare a short trip closure report.

## 6. Safety rule
The trip leader may pause or change an activity when weather, local authority instructions, health concerns or other conditions create a safety risk. Never promise that a route or activity will operate regardless of conditions.
