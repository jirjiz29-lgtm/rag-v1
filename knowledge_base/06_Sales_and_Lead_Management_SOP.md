# GypsyCircle – Sales and Lead Management SOP
Document type: Sales SOP
Status: Proposed workflow

## 1. Objective
Move suitable travel enquiries from first contact to an informed booking decision without misleading or pressuring customers.

## 2. Lead fields
Recommended CRM or spreadsheet fields:
- gc_name
- gc_phone
- gc_destination
- gc_travel_month
- gc_pax
- gc_budget
- gc_source
- gc_status
- gc_owner
- gc_next_followup
- gc_notes

## 3. Lead qualification
Understand:
1. Where does the customer want to travel?
2. When can they travel?
3. How many people are travelling?
4. What is their approximate budget?
5. Are they interested in a fixed departure or a customized trip?
6. Are there important constraints such as age, mobility, dietary needs or room preferences?

## 4. Sales stages
New enquiry → Contacted → Qualified → Itinerary shared → Follow-up → Booking pending → Payment received and verified → Confirmed → Travel completed → Feedback requested.

Use a separate status for Lost, Not eligible or Follow-up later.

## 5. Sales conversation
- Recommend a trip that matches the customer's dates and budget.
- Explain what is included and excluded.
- State whether transport starts from a specific city and whether travel to that city is included.
- Clarify room sharing, additional charges and optional activities.
- Share only current, approved pricing and availability.
- Send payment instructions through an approved company channel.

## 6. Booking confirmation
A booking is confirmed only after the required payment is verified and the company has issued written confirmation under its approved booking process. A screenshot alone should not be treated as proof of cleared funds.

## 7. Follow-up
Record every follow-up and avoid excessive messaging. Stop promotional follow-ups when a person opts out.

## 8. Performance metrics
Track qualified leads, response time, itinerary-to-booking conversion, booking value, cancellation rate, lead source and revenue by departure.
