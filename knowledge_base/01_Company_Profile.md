# GypsyCircle – Company Profile
Document type: Company profile
Status: Draft for internal review
Last updated: 31 October 2026

## 1. Overview
GypsyCircle is a travel company focused on budget-friendly group travel and curated trip experiences across India. Its intended customer segments include college students and working professionals who want to explore destinations through organized group departures.

## 2. Core offering
- Domestic group trips and fixed-departure packages.
- Destination-based itineraries, including Himalayan and other Indian travel experiences.
- Trip coordination covering accommodation, transport, sightseeing and selected meals, depending on the package.
- Travel information and customer assistance before and during a trip.

## 3. Brand promise
Make group travel easier to plan and more accessible through clear package information, coordinated logistics and a welcoming group experience.

## 4. Customer segments
- College students and young travellers.
- Working professionals seeking short breaks or planned holidays.
- Small groups looking for organized domestic travel.

## 5. Operating principles
1. Be transparent about inclusions, exclusions, price and trip conditions.
2. Do not promise services that suppliers have not confirmed.
3. Prioritize traveller safety and practical communication.
4. Keep customer information confidential.
5. Escalate operational, safety and payment issues to the responsible manager.
