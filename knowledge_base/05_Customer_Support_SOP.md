# GypsyCircle – Customer Support SOP
Document type: Customer support procedure
Status: Proposed workflow

## 1. Objective
Provide clear, consistent and documented support from the first enquiry through post-trip feedback.

## 2. Enquiry handling
Capture the following where relevant:
- Customer name
- Phone number and preferred contact channel
- Destination
- Preferred travel month or departure date
- Number of travellers
- Approximate budget
- Specific questions or requirements

Do not request sensitive identity or payment information unless it is necessary for a confirmed booking and an approved secure process exists.

## 3. Response workflow
1. Acknowledge the enquiry.
2. Understand destination, dates, group size and budget.
3. Share the latest approved itinerary and package details.
4. Explain inclusions, exclusions, payment schedule and applicable terms.
5. Record the next follow-up date and owner.
6. Update the lead tracker after every meaningful interaction.

## 4. Complaint handling
1. Listen without interrupting or blaming the customer.
2. Confirm the issue and relevant booking details.
3. Record the facts, dates, evidence and requested resolution.
4. Check the confirmed itinerary, supplier commitments and approved policy.
5. Escalate refund, safety, legal or high-value disputes to management.
6. Give the customer a realistic update timeline.
7. Record the final resolution.

## 5. Response standards
Management should define official support hours and service-level targets. Until approved, staff must not promise a fixed response time.

## 6. Escalation triggers
Immediately escalate safety incidents, medical emergencies, missing travellers, suspected fraud, threats, data exposure and disputes involving significant financial or legal risk.

## 7. Communication rules
Never invent availability, supplier confirmation, refund approval or a policy exception. If information is uncertain, say it is being checked and follow up.
