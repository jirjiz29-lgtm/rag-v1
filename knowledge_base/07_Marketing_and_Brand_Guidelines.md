# GypsyCircle – Marketing and Brand Guidelines
Document type: Marketing guidelines
Status: Proposed brand operations draft

## 1. Positioning
GypsyCircle focuses on accessible, organized group travel and curated experiences across India, particularly for college students and working professionals.

## 2. Brand communication
The tone should be:
- Clear and approachable.
- Informative rather than exaggerated.
- Adventure-oriented but responsible.
- Transparent about price, difficulty, logistics and limitations.

## 3. Content pillars
1. Destination guides and practical travel information.
2. Fixed-departure announcements.
3. Itinerary breakdowns and package inclusions.
4. Real trip experiences and traveller stories, with consent.
5. Safety, packing and preparation tips.
6. Customer reviews and community content, used with permission.

## 4. Package promotion checklist
Every package post or landing page should verify:
- Destination and departure dates.
- Duration and starting/ending point.
- Price and what it covers.
- Accommodation and transport details.
- Meals and activities included.
- Exclusions and optional costs.
- Booking and cancellation terms.
- Availability and last verification date.

## 5. Advertising and claims
- Do not use fake testimonials, fabricated discounts or false scarcity.
- Do not claim a trip is guaranteed where weather, permits or suppliers can affect operations.
- Obtain permission before using customer images, videos or testimonials.
- Ensure promotional claims match the approved package document.

## 6. Lead capture
Use a clear call to action and collect only information needed to respond to the enquiry. Store leads in an approved tracker with access limited to relevant team members.

## 7. Approval
Management should approve package prices, discount campaigns, influencer commitments and material changes to brand claims before publication.
