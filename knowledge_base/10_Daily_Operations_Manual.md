# GypsyCircle – Daily Operations Manual
Document type: Daily operations reference
Status: Proposed workflow

## 1. Daily priorities
At the start of each working day, review:
- New and unanswered travel enquiries.
- Follow-ups due today.
- Bookings awaiting payment verification.
- Upcoming departures and supplier confirmations.
- Customer issues and unresolved complaints.
- Content or campaign approvals due.

## 2. Suggested daily workflow
1. Review the lead tracker and assign enquiry owners.
2. Respond to priority customer enquiries.
3. Update booking and payment status based on verified records.
4. Check upcoming departure readiness.
5. Confirm outstanding supplier details in writing.
6. Review scheduled marketing content and verify package facts.
7. Record decisions, blockers and next actions.

## 3. Departure readiness review
For each upcoming trip, verify the itinerary, traveller list, supplier confirmations, transport, accommodation, payment status, trip leader, emergency contacts and relevant permits or local restrictions.

## 4. Record keeping
Maintain a single approved location for:
- Current package itineraries.
- Supplier quotations and confirmations.
- Booking records and payment reconciliation.
- Customer support cases.
- Trip reports and incident records.
- Approved marketing assets and package claims.

## 5. End-of-day handover
Record completed work, pending enquiries, unresolved customer issues, supplier follow-ups, upcoming deadlines and the person responsible for each action.

## 6. Management review
Management should periodically review lead conversion, booking revenue, trip profitability, supplier performance, customer feedback, cancellations and operational incidents.

## 7. Important control
If a document conflicts with a newer approved policy or booking-specific written confirmation, do not guess which rule applies. Escalate the conflict to management and record the decision.
