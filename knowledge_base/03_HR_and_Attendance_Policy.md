# GypsyCircle – HR, Attendance and Leave Policy
Document type: HR policy
Status: Draft – approval required before implementation

## 1. Scope
Applies to employees and interns. Freelancers, trip leaders and contractors may have separate contractual terms.

## 2. Working schedule
The manager must communicate each role's working days, expected hours, break arrangements and attendance method in writing. No fixed schedule is established by this draft.

## 3. Attendance
- Team members should inform their reporting manager if they expect to be late or absent.
- Planned leave should be requested in advance through the designated channel.
- Emergency absence should be reported as soon as reasonably possible.
- Attendance records must be accurate; no one should mark attendance for another person.

## 4. Leave request workflow
1. Team member sends the requested dates and reason to the reporting manager.
2. Manager checks workload, trip dates and handover requirements.
3. Manager approves or declines the request in writing.
4. Approved leave is recorded in the company tracker.

## 5. Leave entitlement
Insert approved annual, casual, sick and other leave entitlements here after checking employment contracts and applicable law. Do not assume a number of leave days from this template.

## 6. Extended absence and emergencies
The team member should provide an update and expected return date when possible. Management should arrange coverage for customer enquiries and active departures.

## 7. Records and privacy
Keep attendance and leave records access-controlled. Collect only information required for legitimate HR administration.
