# GypsyCircle – Vendor Management SOP
Document type: Supplier and vendor procedure
Status: Proposed workflow

## 1. Objective
Build a reliable supplier network for accommodation, transport, guides, activities and other trip services.

## 2. Vendor onboarding
Collect and verify, as appropriate:
- Business or provider name and primary contact.
- Service area and service description.
- Business registration or licence where applicable.
- References and relevant experience.
- Rate card, taxes and payment terms.
- Cancellation and amendment terms.
- Emergency contact and escalation details.
- Insurance or safety documentation where relevant.

## 3. Supplier evaluation
Assess service quality, reliability, safety, communication, pricing transparency, location, capacity and complaint history. Do not select solely on the lowest price.

## 4. Booking confirmation
Before confirming a service, obtain written agreement on:
- Service dates and timings.
- Quantity, capacity and room or vehicle category.
- Agreed rate and taxes.
- Inclusions and exclusions.
- Advance, balance and due dates.
- Cancellation and refund terms.
- Contact person for service delivery.

## 5. Payment controls
Record approved quotations, invoices, payment proofs and outstanding balances. Payments should follow the company's authorized approval process. This document does not set payment limits or credit terms.

## 6. Vendor performance
After each trip, record punctuality, service quality, issue resolution, traveller feedback and any billing discrepancy.

## 7. Escalation
Escalate safety concerns, suspected fraud, unapproved price changes, service non-delivery and repeated failures to management. Keep a backup supplier option for critical services where practical.
