# GypsyCircle – Team Onboarding Guide
Document type: New team member onboarding
Status: Proposed workflow

## 1. Welcome and role clarity
Every new team member should receive a written role description, reporting contact, responsibilities, access requirements and performance expectations.

## 2. First-week orientation
- Understand GypsyCircle's customer segments and travel products.
- Review the company profile and approved brand guidelines.
- Learn the enquiry-to-booking process.
- Review customer support and escalation procedures.
- Understand data privacy and approved communication channels.
- Learn how trip itineraries and supplier confirmations are stored.

## 3. Role-based training
Sales: lead qualification, package explanation, follow-up and CRM updates.
Marketing: content pillars, approval workflow, claims and consent.
Operations: itinerary checks, supplier confirmations, passenger coordination and incident escalation.
Trip leaders: group briefing, attendance, safety communication and trip reporting.

## 4. Access and security
Provide only the system access required for the role. Use individual accounts where possible; do not share passwords. Remove access when a person leaves or changes roles.

## 5. Readiness checklist
A team member should demonstrate that they can:
- Find the latest approved itinerary.
- Explain package inclusions and exclusions.
- Locate the relevant SOP.
- Update the lead or operations tracker.
- Escalate an issue to the correct person.
- Handle customer information responsibly.

## 6. Training sign-off
Record the trainer, training date, topics completed and any follow-up training required.
