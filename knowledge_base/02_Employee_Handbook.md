# GypsyCircle – Employee and Team Handbook
Document type: Team handbook
Status: Proposed draft – requires management and legal review

## 1. Purpose
This handbook sets expectations for employees, interns, freelancers, trip leaders and other people working with GypsyCircle.

## 2. Professional conduct
- Communicate respectfully with customers, colleagues and suppliers.
- Do not discriminate, harass, threaten or intimidate anyone.
- Be punctual for assigned work, meetings and trip duties.
- Protect customer, supplier and company information.
- Do not make unauthorized commitments on pricing, refunds, upgrades or compensation.

## 3. Communication
- Use company-approved communication channels for work.
- Keep trip-related decisions and supplier confirmations in a traceable written channel.
- Respond within the response time agreed with the team lead.
- Escalate urgent safety incidents immediately.

## 4. Customer-facing conduct
- Share accurate package information.
- Never misrepresent accommodation, transport, meals, difficulty level or inclusions.
- Do not pressure customers with false scarcity or fabricated discounts.
- Treat complaints as issues to resolve, not arguments to win.

## 5. Confidentiality and data
- Access only the customer and business information needed for assigned work.
- Do not share customer phone numbers, identity documents, payment details or rooming lists outside authorized work.
- Do not upload confidential data to public AI tools without management approval.
- Return or securely delete company data when access ends, subject to applicable record-retention requirements.

## 6. Attendance, leave and compensation
Specific working hours, leave entitlements, holidays, payroll dates, notice periods and benefits must be inserted from approved employment agreements and applicable law. This draft does not establish those terms.

## 7. Disciplinary matters
Concerns should be documented and reviewed fairly by management. Any disciplinary process must follow the applicable contract and law.
